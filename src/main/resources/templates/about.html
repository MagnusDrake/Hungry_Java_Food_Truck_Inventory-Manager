Our Story

Welcome to The Hungry Java! We started as a small project to see if software developers could cook as well as they code. It turns out, debugging a soup is a lot like debugging Java—it's all about finding the right ingredients.

We specialize in gourmet street food using locally sourced ingredients from Forest City farmers and high-quality wholesale goods.

Inventory Management System

This application is the engine that keeps our truck running. It was custom-built using Spring Boot to manage our unique needs:

  • Ingredient Tracking Real-time counts of beef, buns, and veggies.
  • Sourcing Logic Differentiates between Local vs. Wholesale vendors.
  • Cost Analysis Calculates Labor Cost per item to ensure profitability.
